Santali — 12 Starter Sentences

There are now 12 lines, divided into three small levels so a beginner can build confidence gradually.

HomeAlphabetWords12 SentencesPractice7-Day Plan

How to practise each sentence

1. SeeLook at native script.
2. MeaningKnow what it says.
3. ListenPlay audio once.
4. EchoRepeat in chunks.
5. SpeakSay it without looking.

Level 1 — Greeting & identity

Read → understand → listen → echo → speak.

Sentence 1
ᱡᱚᱦᱟᱨ ᱾
johar.
Hello.
Sentence 2
ᱪᱮᱫ ᱞᱮᱠᱟ ᱢᱮᱱᱟᱢᱟ?
ched leka menama?
How are you?
Sentence 3
ᱟᱢᱟᱜ ᱧᱩᱛᱩᱢ ᱪᱮᱫ?
amag nyutum ched?
What is your name?
Sentence 4
ᱤᱧᱟᱜ ᱧᱩᱛᱩᱢ ᱫᱚ ___ ᱾
iñag nyutum do ___.
My name is ___.

Level 2 — Needs & objects

Read → understand → listen → echo → speak.

Sentence 5
ᱟᱢ ᱫᱚ ᱚᱠᱟᱨᱮᱢ ᱛᱟᱦᱮᱱᱟ?
am do okarem tahena?
Where do you live?
Sentence 6
ᱤᱧ ᱫᱚ ᱨᱟᱭᱨᱚᱢᱯᱩᱨ ᱨᱤᱧ ᱛᱟᱦᱮᱱᱟ ᱾
iñ do Rairangpur riñ tahena.
I live in Rairangpur.
Sentence 7
ᱟᱢ ᱫᱚ ᱚᱠᱟ ᱪᱟᱱᱚᱪ ᱨᱮᱢ ᱚᱞᱚᱜ ᱠᱟᱱᱟ?
am do oka canoc rem olog kana?
In which class do you study?
Sentence 8
ᱤᱧ ᱫᱚ ᱛᱩᱨᱩᱭ ᱪᱟᱱᱚᱪ ᱨᱤᱧ ᱚᱞᱚᱜ ᱠᱟᱱᱟ ᱾
iñ do turuy canoc riñ olog kana.
I study in class VI.

Level 3 — Everyday use

Read → understand → listen → echo → speak.

Sentence 9
ᱟᱢ ᱟᱭᱚᱼᱵᱟᱵᱟᱣᱟᱜ ᱧᱩᱛᱩᱢ ᱪᱮᱫ?
am ayo-babāwag nyutum ched?
What are your parents' names?
Sentence 10
ᱥᱟᱹᱜᱩᱱ ᱫᱟᱨᱟᱢ ᱾
sagun daram.
Welcome.
Sentence 11
ᱥᱟᱨᱦᱟᱣ ᱾
sarhao.
Thank you.
Sentence 12
ᱫᱟᱭᱟ ᱠᱟᱛᱮ ᱾
daya kate.
Please.

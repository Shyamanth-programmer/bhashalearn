Hindi — 12 Starter Sentences

There are now 12 lines, divided into three small levels so a beginner can build confidence gradually.

HomeAlphabetWords12 SentencesPractice7-Day Plan

How to practise each sentence

1. SeeLook at native script.
2. MeaningKnow what it says.
3. ListenPlay audio once.
4. EchoRepeat in chunks.
5. SpeakSay it without looking.

Level 1 — Greeting & identity

Read → understand → listen → echo → speak.

Sentence 1
नमस्ते।
namaste.
Hello.
Sentence 2
मेरा नाम ___ है।
merā nām ___ hai.
My name is ___.
Sentence 3
आपका नाम क्या है?
āpkā nām kyā hai?
What is your name?
Sentence 4
मैं ठीक हूँ।
maĩ ṭhīk hū̃.
I am fine.

Level 2 — Needs & objects

Read → understand → listen → echo → speak.

Sentence 5
मुझे पानी चाहिए।
mujhe pānī chāhiye.
I need water.
Sentence 6
यह एक किताब है।
yah ek kitāb hai.
This is a book.
Sentence 7
यह मेरा घर है।
yah merā ghar hai.
This is my house.
Sentence 8
मैं स्कूल जाता हूँ।
maĩ skūl jātā hū̃.
I go to school.

Level 3 — Everyday use

Read → understand → listen → echo → speak.

Sentence 9
वह मेरा दोस्त है।
vah merā dost hai.
He is my friend.
Sentence 10
मैं पानी पीता हूँ।
maĩ pānī pītā hū̃.
I drink water.
Sentence 11
यह लाल है।
yah lāl hai.
This is red.
Sentence 12
धन्यवाद।
dhanyavād.
Thank you.

Maithili — 12 Starter Sentences

There are now 12 lines, divided into three small levels so a beginner can build confidence gradually.

HomeAlphabetWords12 SentencesPractice7-Day Plan

How to practise each sentence

1. SeeLook at native script.
2. MeaningKnow what it says.
3. ListenPlay audio once.
4. EchoRepeat in chunks.
5. SpeakSay it without looking.

Level 1 — Greeting & identity

Read → understand → listen → echo → speak.

Sentence 1
प्रणाम।
praṇām.
Hello.
Sentence 2
हमर नाम ___ अछि।
hamar nām ___ achi.
My name is ___.
Sentence 3
अहाँक नाम की अछि?
ahā̃k nām kī achi?
What is your name?
Sentence 4
हम नीक छी।
ham nīk chhī.
I am fine.

Level 2 — Needs & objects

Read → understand → listen → echo → speak.

Sentence 5
हमरा पानि चाही।
hamrā pāni chāhī.
I need water.
Sentence 6
ई किताब अछि।
ī kitāb achi.
This is a book.
Sentence 7
ई हमर घर अछि।
ī hamar ghar achi.
This is my house.
Sentence 8
हम विद्यालय जाइ छी।
ham vidyālay jāi chhī.
I go to school.

Level 3 — Everyday use

Read → understand → listen → echo → speak.

Sentence 9
ओ हमर मित्र अछि।
o hamar mitra achi.
He/She is my friend.
Sentence 10
हम पानि पिबैत छी।
ham pāni pibait chhī.
I drink water.
Sentence 11
आइ बिहान नीक अछि।
āi bihān nīk achi.
This morning is good.
Sentence 12
धन्यवाद।
dhanyavād.
Thank you.

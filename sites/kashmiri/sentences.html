Kashmiri — 12 Starter Sentences

There are now 12 lines, divided into three small levels so a beginner can build confidence gradually.

HomeAlphabetWords12 SentencesPractice7-Day Plan

How to practise each sentence

1. SeeLook at native script.
2. MeaningKnow what it says.
3. ListenPlay audio once.
4. EchoRepeat in chunks.
5. SpeakSay it without looking.

Level 1 — Greeting & identity

Read → understand → listen → echo → speak.

Sentence 1
آداب۔
ādāb.
Hello.
Sentence 2
توہہِ چھوا وارے؟
toh chiv väri?
How are you?
Sentence 3
اہنسا، مہربانی۔
ahansa, meherbāni.
Fine, thank you.
Sentence 4
ژے کیاہ چھُے ناو؟
tse kyāh chuy nāv?
What is your name?

Level 2 — Needs & objects

Read → understand → listen → echo → speak.

Sentence 5
بہٕ چھُس ___۔
be chus ___.
My name is ___.
Sentence 6
مہربانی۔
meherbāni.
Thank you.
Sentence 7
بَلے لَگَے۔
balai lagai.
Please.
Sentence 8
یِہ کیاہ چھُ؟
yi kyāh chu?
What is this?

Level 3 — Everyday use

Read → understand → listen → echo → speak.

Sentence 9
مےٚ چھُنہٕ فِکری تَگان۔
me chune fikri tagān.
I don't understand.
Sentence 10
ژٕ چھُکھا اَنگرٛیٖز بُلان؟
tse chukh angrez bolān?
Do you speak English?
Sentence 11
اَکھ، زٕ، ترٛےٚ۔
akh, ze, tre.
One, two, three.
Sentence 12
خُدا حافِظ۔
khudā hāfiz.
Goodbye.

Odia — 12 Starter Sentences

There are now 12 lines, divided into three small levels so a beginner can build confidence gradually.

HomeAlphabetWords12 SentencesPractice7-Day Plan

How to practise each sentence

1. SeeLook at native script.
2. MeaningKnow what it says.
3. ListenPlay audio once.
4. EchoRepeat in chunks.
5. SpeakSay it without looking.

Level 1 — Greeting & identity

Read → understand → listen → echo → speak.

Sentence 1
ନମସ୍କାର।
namaskāra.
Hello.
Sentence 2
ମୋ ନାମ ___।
mo nāma ___.
My name is ___.
Sentence 3
ଆପଣଙ୍କ ନାମ କଣ?
āpaṇaṅka nāma kaṇa?
What is your name?
Sentence 4
ମୁଁ ଭଲ ଅଛି।
mũ bhala achhi.
I am fine.

Level 2 — Needs & objects

Read → understand → listen → echo → speak.

Sentence 5
ମୋତେ ପାଣି ଦରକାର।
mote pāṇi darkār.
I need water.
Sentence 6
ଏହା ଏକ ବହି।
ehā eka bahi.
This is a book.
Sentence 7
ଏହା ମୋ ଘର।
ehā mo ghara.
This is my house.
Sentence 8
ମୁଁ ସ୍କୁଲକୁ ଯାଏ।
mũ skulaku jāe.
I go to school.

Level 3 — Everyday use

Read → understand → listen → echo → speak.

Sentence 9
ସେ ମୋ ବନ୍ଧୁ।
se mo bandhu.
He/She is my friend.
Sentence 10
ମୁଁ ପାଣି ପିଏ।
mũ pāṇi pie.
I drink water.
Sentence 11
ଏହା ଲାଲ।
ehā lāla.
This is red.
Sentence 12
ଧନ୍ୟବାଦ।
dhanyabāda.
Thank you.

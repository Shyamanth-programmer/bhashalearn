Urdu — 12 Starter Sentences

There are now 12 lines, divided into three small levels so a beginner can build confidence gradually.

HomeAlphabetWords12 SentencesPractice7-Day Plan

How to practise each sentence

1. SeeLook at native script.
2. MeaningKnow what it says.
3. ListenPlay audio once.
4. EchoRepeat in chunks.
5. SpeakSay it without looking.

Level 1 — Greeting & identity

Read → understand → listen → echo → speak.

Sentence 1
السلام علیکم۔
assalāmu alaikum.
Hello.
Sentence 2
میرا نام ___ ہے۔
merā nām ___ hai.
My name is ___.
Sentence 3
آپ کا نام کیا ہے؟
āp kā nām kyā hai?
What is your name?
Sentence 4
میں ٹھیک ہوں۔
maĩ ṭhīk hū̃.
I am fine.

Level 2 — Needs & objects

Read → understand → listen → echo → speak.

Sentence 5
مجھے پانی چاہیے۔
mujhe pānī chāhiye.
I need water.
Sentence 6
یہ ایک کتاب ہے۔
yeh ek kitāb hai.
This is a book.
Sentence 7
یہ میرا گھر ہے۔
yeh merā ghar hai.
This is my house.
Sentence 8
میں اسکول جاتا ہوں۔
maĩ iskūl jātā hū̃.
I go to school.

Level 3 — Everyday use

Read → understand → listen → echo → speak.

Sentence 9
وہ میرا دوست ہے۔
woh merā dost hai.
He is my friend.
Sentence 10
میں پانی پیتا ہوں۔
maĩ pānī pītā hū̃.
I drink water.
Sentence 11
یہ لال ہے۔
yeh lāl hai.
This is red.
Sentence 12
شکریہ۔
shukriyā.
Thank you.

Sindhi — 12 Starter Sentences

There are now 12 lines, divided into three small levels so a beginner can build confidence gradually.

HomeAlphabetWords12 SentencesPractice7-Day Plan

How to practise each sentence

1. SeeLook at native script.
2. MeaningKnow what it says.
3. ListenPlay audio once.
4. EchoRepeat in chunks.
5. SpeakSay it without looking.

Level 1 — Greeting & identity

Read → understand → listen → echo → speak.

Sentence 1
سلام۔
salām.
Hello.
Sentence 2
ڪيئن آهيو؟
ke-in āhyo?
How are you?
Sentence 3
مان ٺيڪ آهيان، مهرباني۔
mān ṭhīk āhiyān, mehrbāni.
I am fine, thank you.
Sentence 4
توهان جو نالو ڇا آهي؟
tohān jo nālo chhā āhe?
What is your name?

Level 2 — Needs & objects

Read → understand → listen → echo → speak.

Sentence 5
منهنجو نالو ___ آهي۔
munhinjo nālo ___ āhe.
My name is ___.
Sentence 6
توهان ڪٿان جا آهيو؟
tohān kithān jā āhyo?
Where are you from?
Sentence 7
مان ___ جو آهيان۔
mān ___ jo āhiyān.
I am from ___.
Sentence 8
مون کي خبر ناهي۔
mun khe khabar nāhi.
I don't know.

Level 3 — Everyday use

Read → understand → listen → echo → speak.

Sentence 9
مان نـ سمجهيس۔
mān na samjhis.
I don't understand.
Sentence 10
هن جو اگھ ڇا آهي؟
hin jo agh chhā āhe?
How much is this?
Sentence 11
معاف ڪجو۔
maāf kaju.
Excuse me / sorry.
Sentence 12
مهرباني۔
mehrbāni.
Thank you.

Telugu — 12 Starter Sentences

There are now 12 lines, divided into three small levels so a beginner can build confidence gradually.

HomeAlphabetWords12 SentencesPractice7-Day Plan

How to practise each sentence

1. SeeLook at native script.
2. MeaningKnow what it says.
3. ListenPlay audio once.
4. EchoRepeat in chunks.
5. SpeakSay it without looking.

Level 1 — Greeting & identity

Read → understand → listen → echo → speak.

Sentence 1
నమస్కారం।
namaskāram.
Hello.
Sentence 2
నా పేరు ___।
nā pēru ___.
My name is ___.
Sentence 3
మీ పేరు ఏమిటి?
mī pēru ēmiṭi?
What is your name?
Sentence 4
నేను బాగున్నాను।
nēnu bāgunnānu.
I am fine.

Level 2 — Needs & objects

Read → understand → listen → echo → speak.

Sentence 5
నాకు నీళ్లు కావాలి।
nāku nīḷḷu kāvāli.
I need water.
Sentence 6
ఇది ఒక పుస్తకం।
idi oka pustakam.
This is a book.
Sentence 7
ఇది నా ఇల్లు।
idi nā illu.
This is my house.
Sentence 8
నేను పాఠశాలకు వెళ్తాను।
nēnu pāṭhaśālaku veḷtānu.
I go to school.

Level 3 — Everyday use

Read → understand → listen → echo → speak.

Sentence 9
అతను నా స్నేహితుడు।
atanu nā snēhituḍu.
He is my friend.
Sentence 10
నేను నీళ్లు తాగుతాను।
nēnu nīḷḷu tāgutānu.
I drink water.
Sentence 11
ఇది ఎరుపు।
idi erupu.
This is red.
Sentence 12
ధన్యవాదాలు।
dhanyavādālu.
Thank you.

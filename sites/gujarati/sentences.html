Gujarati — 12 Starter Sentences

There are now 12 lines, divided into three small levels so a beginner can build confidence gradually.

HomeAlphabetWords12 SentencesPractice7-Day Plan

How to practise each sentence

1. SeeLook at native script.
2. MeaningKnow what it says.
3. ListenPlay audio once.
4. EchoRepeat in chunks.
5. SpeakSay it without looking.

Level 1 — Greeting & identity

Read → understand → listen → echo → speak.

Sentence 1
નમસ્તે।
namaste.
Hello.
Sentence 2
મારું નામ ___ છે.
māruṁ nām ___ chhe.
My name is ___.
Sentence 3
તમારું નામ શું છે?
tamāruṁ nām shuṁ chhe?
What is your name?
Sentence 4
હું મજામાં છું.
huṁ majāmā̃ chuṁ.
I am fine.

Level 2 — Needs & objects

Read → understand → listen → echo → speak.

Sentence 5
મને પાણી જોઈએ.
mane pāṇī joīe.
I need water.
Sentence 6
આ પુસ્તક છે.
ā pustak chhe.
This is a book.
Sentence 7
આ મારું ઘર છે.
ā māruṁ ghar chhe.
This is my house.
Sentence 8
હું શાળાએ જાઉં છું.
huṁ shāḷāe jāuṁ chuṁ.
I go to school.

Level 3 — Everyday use

Read → understand → listen → echo → speak.

Sentence 9
તે મારો મિત્ર છે.
te māro mitra chhe.
He is my friend.
Sentence 10
હું પાણી પીઉં છું.
huṁ pāṇī pīuṁ chuṁ.
I drink water.
Sentence 11
આ લાલ છે.
ā lāl chhe.
This is red.
Sentence 12
આભાર।
ābhār.
Thank you.

Bodo — 12 Starter Sentences

There are now 12 lines, divided into three small levels so a beginner can build confidence gradually.

HomeAlphabetWords12 SentencesPractice7-Day Plan

How to practise each sentence

1. SeeLook at native script.
2. MeaningKnow what it says.
3. ListenPlay audio once.
4. EchoRepeat in chunks.
5. SpeakSay it without looking.

Level 1 — Greeting & identity

Read → understand → listen → echo → speak.

Sentence 1
खुलुमबाय!
khulumbai!
Hello!
Sentence 2
आदर खालामनाय।
ador khalamnai.
Welcome.
Sentence 3
मा खबोर?
ma khobor?
How are you?
Sentence 4
नोंनि मुंआ मा?
nwngni munga ma?
What is your name?

Level 2 — Needs & objects

Read → understand → listen → echo → speak.

Sentence 5
आंनि मुं ___।
angni mung ___.
My name is ___.
Sentence 6
गोजोन फुंबिलि!
gwjwn fungbili!
Good morning!
Sentence 7
गोजोन हर।
gwjwn hor.
Good night.
Sentence 8
आं मन्थिया।
ang mwnthiya.
I don't understand.

Level 3 — Everyday use

Read → understand → listen → echo → speak.

Sentence 9
आं फैबाय।
ang phaibai.
I have come.
Sentence 10
नों ओंखाम जाबाय?
nwng onkham jabai?
Have you eaten?
Sentence 11
से, न्वि, थाम।
se, nwi, tham.
One, two, three.
Sentence 12
साबायखोर।
sabaykhor.
Thank you.

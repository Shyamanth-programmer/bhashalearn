Sanskrit — 12 Starter Sentences

There are now 12 lines, divided into three small levels so a beginner can build confidence gradually.

HomeAlphabetWords12 SentencesPractice7-Day Plan

How to practise each sentence

1. SeeLook at native script.
2. MeaningKnow what it says.
3. ListenPlay audio once.
4. EchoRepeat in chunks.
5. SpeakSay it without looking.

Level 1 — Greeting & identity

Read → understand → listen → echo → speak.

Sentence 1
नमस्ते।
namaste.
Greetings.
Sentence 2
मम नाम ___।
mama nāma ___.
My name is ___.
Sentence 3
भवतः नाम किम्?
bhavataḥ nāma kim?
What is your name?
Sentence 4
अहं कुशली अस्मि।
ahaṁ kuśalī asmi.
I am well.

Level 2 — Needs & objects

Read → understand → listen → echo → speak.

Sentence 5
मम जलम् आवश्यकम्।
mama jalam āvaśyakam.
I need water.
Sentence 6
इदं पुस्तकम् अस्ति।
idaṁ pustakam asti.
This is a book.
Sentence 7
इदं मम गृहम् अस्ति।
idaṁ mama gr̥ham asti.
This is my house.
Sentence 8
अहं विद्यालयं गच्छामि।
ahaṁ vidyālayaṁ gacchāmi.
I go to school.

Level 3 — Everyday use

Read → understand → listen → echo → speak.

Sentence 9
सः मम मित्रम् अस्ति।
saḥ mama mitram asti.
He is my friend.
Sentence 10
अहं जलं पिबामि।
ahaṁ jalaṁ pibāmi.
I drink water.
Sentence 11
इदं रक्तम् अस्ति।
idaṁ raktam asti.
This is red.
Sentence 12
धन्यवादः।
dhanyavādaḥ.
Thank you.

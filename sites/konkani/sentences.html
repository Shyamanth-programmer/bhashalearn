Konkani — 12 Starter Sentences

There are now 12 lines, divided into three small levels so a beginner can build confidence gradually.

HomeAlphabetWords12 SentencesPractice7-Day Plan

How to practise each sentence

1. SeeLook at native script.
2. MeaningKnow what it says.
3. ListenPlay audio once.
4. EchoRepeat in chunks.
5. SpeakSay it without looking.

Level 1 — Greeting & identity

Read → understand → listen → echo → speak.

Sentence 1
नमस्कार।
namaskār.
Hello.
Sentence 2
म्हजें नांव ___ आसा।
mhajẽ nāv ___ āsā.
My name is ___.
Sentence 3
तुमचें नांव कितें?
tumchem nāv kitem?
What is your name?
Sentence 4
हांव बरो आसा।
hā̃v boro āsā.
I am fine.

Level 2 — Needs & objects

Read → understand → listen → echo → speak.

Sentence 5
म्हाका उदक जाय।
mhākā udak zāi.
I need water.
Sentence 6
हें पुस्तक आसा।
hem pustak āsā.
This is a book.
Sentence 7
हें म्हजें घर आसा।
hem mhajẽ ghar āsā.
This is my house.
Sentence 8
हांव शाळेंत वता।
hā̃v shāḷent vatā.
I go to school.

Level 3 — Everyday use

Read → understand → listen → echo → speak.

Sentence 9
तो म्हजो इश्ट।
to mhajo iṣṭ.
He is my friend.
Sentence 10
उदक दिया।
udak diyā.
Water, please.
Sentence 11
कितलें?
kitlem?
How much?
Sentence 12
देवाचें बरें करूं।
devāchem borem karum.
Thank you.

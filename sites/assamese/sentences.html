Assamese — 12 Starter Sentences

There are now 12 lines, divided into three small levels so a beginner can build confidence gradually.

HomeAlphabetWords12 SentencesPractice7-Day Plan

How to practise each sentence

1. SeeLook at native script.
2. MeaningKnow what it says.
3. ListenPlay audio once.
4. EchoRepeat in chunks.
5. SpeakSay it without looking.

Level 1 — Greeting & identity

Read → understand → listen → echo → speak.

Sentence 1
নমস্কাৰ।
nomoskār.
Hello.
Sentence 2
মোৰ নাম ___।
mur nām ___.
My name is ___.
Sentence 3
আপোনাৰ নাম কি?
āponār nām ki?
What is your name?
Sentence 4
মই ভাল আছোঁ।
moi bhāl āsõ.
I am fine.

Level 2 — Needs & objects

Read → understand → listen → echo → speak.

Sentence 5
মোক পানী লাগে।
mok pānī lāge.
I need water.
Sentence 6
এইটো কিতাপ।
eiṭo kitāp.
This is a book.
Sentence 7
এইটো মোৰ ঘৰ।
eiṭo mur ghar.
This is my house.
Sentence 8
মই বিদ্যালয়লৈ যাওঁ।
moi bidyāloyoloi jāõ.
I go to school.

Level 3 — Everyday use

Read → understand → listen → echo → speak.

Sentence 9
মোৰ এজন বন্ধু আছে।
mur ejon bondhu āse.
I have a friend.
Sentence 10
মই পানী খাওঁ।
moi pānī khāõ.
I drink water.
Sentence 11
এইটো ৰঙা।
eiṭo roṅā.
This is red.
Sentence 12
ধন্যবাদ।
dhonyobād.
Thank you.

Punjabi — 12 Starter Sentences

There are now 12 lines, divided into three small levels so a beginner can build confidence gradually.

HomeAlphabetWords12 SentencesPractice7-Day Plan

How to practise each sentence

1. SeeLook at native script.
2. MeaningKnow what it says.
3. ListenPlay audio once.
4. EchoRepeat in chunks.
5. SpeakSay it without looking.

Level 1 — Greeting & identity

Read → understand → listen → echo → speak.

Sentence 1
ਸਤ ਸ੍ਰੀ ਅਕਾਲ।
sat srī akāl.
Hello.
Sentence 2
ਮੇਰਾ ਨਾਮ ___ ਹੈ।
merā nām ___ hai.
My name is ___.
Sentence 3
ਤੁਹਾਡਾ ਨਾਮ ਕੀ ਹੈ?
tuhāḍā nām kī hai?
What is your name?
Sentence 4
ਮੈਂ ਠੀਕ ਹਾਂ।
maĩ ṭhīk hā̃.
I am fine.

Level 2 — Needs & objects

Read → understand → listen → echo → speak.

Sentence 5
ਮੈਨੂੰ ਪਾਣੀ ਚਾਹੀਦਾ ਹੈ।
mainū̃ pāṇī cāhīdā hai.
I need water.
Sentence 6
ਇਹ ਕਿਤਾਬ ਹੈ।
ih kitāb hai.
This is a book.
Sentence 7
ਇਹ ਮੇਰਾ ਘਰ ਹੈ।
ih merā ghar hai.
This is my house.
Sentence 8
ਮੈਂ ਸਕੂਲ ਜਾਂਦਾ ਹਾਂ।
maĩ sakūl jāndā hā̃.
I go to school.

Level 3 — Everyday use

Read → understand → listen → echo → speak.

Sentence 9
ਉਹ ਮੇਰਾ ਦੋਸਤ ਹੈ।
uh merā dost hai.
He is my friend.
Sentence 10
ਮੈਂ ਪਾਣੀ ਪੀਂਦਾ ਹਾਂ।
maĩ pāṇī pīndā hā̃.
I drink water.
Sentence 11
ਇਹ ਲਾਲ ਹੈ।
ih lāl hai.
This is red.
Sentence 12
ਧੰਨਵਾਦ।
dhannavād.
Thank you.

Manipuri — 12 Starter Sentences

There are now 12 lines, divided into three small levels so a beginner can build confidence gradually.

HomeAlphabetWords12 SentencesPractice7-Day Plan

How to practise each sentence

1. SeeLook at native script.
2. MeaningKnow what it says.
3. ListenPlay audio once.
4. EchoRepeat in chunks.
5. SpeakSay it without looking.

Level 1 — Greeting & identity

Read → understand → listen → echo → speak.

Sentence 1
ꯈꯨꯔꯨꯝꯖꯔꯤ।
khurumjari.
Hello.
Sentence 2
ꯅꯥꯍꯀꯤ ꯃꯤꯡ ꯀꯔꯤ ꯀꯧꯏ?
nahakki ming kari kou-i?
What is your name?
Sentence 3
ꯑꯩꯒꯤ ꯃꯤꯡ ___ ꯀꯧꯏ।
eigi ming ___ kou-i.
My name is ___.
Sentence 4
ꯑꯩ ꯐꯖꯩ।
ei phajai.
I am fine.

Level 2 — Needs & objects

Read → understand → listen → echo → speak.

Sentence 5
ꯊꯒꯥꯆꯔꯤ।
thagatchari.
Thank you.
Sentence 6
ꯍꯧꯛꯈꯤ।
houkhi.
Yes.
Sentence 7
ꯅꯥꯠ꯭ꯔꯥꯒꯥ।
nattraga.
No.
Sentence 8
ꯑꯩꯅ ꯏꯁꯤꯡ ꯊꯛꯏ।
eina ishing thak-i.
I drink water.

Level 3 — Everyday use

Read → understand → listen → echo → speak.

Sentence 9

eigi ima.
My mother.
Sentence 10
ꯑꯩꯒꯤ ꯃꯄꯥ।
eigi mapa.
My father.
Sentence 11
Ama, ani, ahum.
ama, ani, ahum.
One, two, three.
Sentence 12
Chatnaba.
chatnaba.

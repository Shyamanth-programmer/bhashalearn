Marathi — 12 Starter Sentences

There are now 12 lines, divided into three small levels so a beginner can build confidence gradually.

HomeAlphabetWords12 SentencesPractice7-Day Plan

How to practise each sentence

1. SeeLook at native script.
2. MeaningKnow what it says.
3. ListenPlay audio once.
4. EchoRepeat in chunks.
5. SpeakSay it without looking.

Level 1 — Greeting & identity

Read → understand → listen → echo → speak.

Sentence 1
नमस्कार।
namaskār.
Hello.
Sentence 2
माझं नाव ___ आहे।
mājhaṁ nāv ___ āhe.
My name is ___.
Sentence 3
तुमचं नाव काय आहे?
tumchaṁ nāv kāy āhe?
What is your name?
Sentence 4
मी ठीक आहे।
mī ṭhīk āhe.
I am fine.

Level 2 — Needs & objects

Read → understand → listen → echo → speak.

Sentence 5
मला पाणी पाहिजे।
malā pāṇī pāhije.
I need water.
Sentence 6
हे पुस्तक आहे।
he pustak āhe.
This is a book.
Sentence 7
हे माझं घर आहे।
he mājhaṁ ghar āhe.
This is my house.
Sentence 8
मी शाळेत जातो।
mī śāḷet jāto.
I go to school.

Level 3 — Everyday use

Read → understand → listen → echo → speak.

Sentence 9
तो माझा मित्र आहे।
to mājhā mitra āhe.
He is my friend.
Sentence 10
मी पाणी पितो।
mī pāṇī pito.
I drink water.
Sentence 11
हे लाल आहे।
he lāl āhe.
This is red.
Sentence 12
धन्यवाद।
dhanyavād.
Thank you.

Kannada — 12 Starter Sentences

There are now 12 lines, divided into three small levels so a beginner can build confidence gradually.

HomeAlphabetWords12 SentencesPractice7-Day Plan

How to practise each sentence

1. SeeLook at native script.
2. MeaningKnow what it says.
3. ListenPlay audio once.
4. EchoRepeat in chunks.
5. SpeakSay it without looking.

Level 1 — Greeting & identity

Read → understand → listen → echo → speak.

Sentence 1
ನಮಸ್ಕಾರ।
namaskāra.
Hello.
Sentence 2
ನನ್ನ ಹೆಸರು ___।
nanna hesaru ___.
My name is ___.
Sentence 3
ನಿಮ್ಮ ಹೆಸರು ಏನು?
nimma hesaru ēnu?
What is your name?
Sentence 4
ನಾನು ಚೆನ್ನಾಗಿದ್ದೇನೆ।
nānu cennāgiddēne.
I am fine.

Level 2 — Needs & objects

Read → understand → listen → echo → speak.

Sentence 5
ನನಗೆ ನೀರು ಬೇಕು।
nanage nīru bēku.
I need water.
Sentence 6
ಇದು ಪುಸ್ತಕ।
idu pustaka.
This is a book.
Sentence 7
ಇದು ನನ್ನ ಮನೆ।
idu nanna mane.
This is my house.
Sentence 8
ನಾನು ಶಾಲೆಗೆ ಹೋಗುತ್ತೇನೆ।
nānu śālege hōguttēne.
I go to school.

Level 3 — Everyday use

Read → understand → listen → echo → speak.

Sentence 9
ಅವನು ನನ್ನ ಸ್ನೇಹಿತ।
avanu nanna snēhita.
He is my friend.
Sentence 10
ನಾನು ನೀರು ಕುಡಿಯುತ್ತೇನೆ।
nānu nīru kuḍiyuttēne.
I drink water.
Sentence 11
ಇದು ಕೆಂಪು।
idu kempu.
This is red.
Sentence 12
ಧನ್ಯವಾದಗಳು।
dhanyavādagaḷu.
Thank you.

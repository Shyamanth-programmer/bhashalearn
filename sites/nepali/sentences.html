Nepali — 12 Starter Sentences

There are now 12 lines, divided into three small levels so a beginner can build confidence gradually.

HomeAlphabetWords12 SentencesPractice7-Day Plan

How to practise each sentence

1. SeeLook at native script.
2. MeaningKnow what it says.
3. ListenPlay audio once.
4. EchoRepeat in chunks.
5. SpeakSay it without looking.

Level 1 — Greeting & identity

Read → understand → listen → echo → speak.

Sentence 1
नमस्ते।
namaste.
Hello.
Sentence 2
मेरो नाम ___ हो।
mero nām ___ ho.
My name is ___.
Sentence 3
तपाईंको नाम के हो?
tapāī̃ko nām ke ho?
What is your name?
Sentence 4
म ठिक छु।
ma ṭhik chu.
I am fine.

Level 2 — Needs & objects

Read → understand → listen → echo → speak.

Sentence 5
मलाई पानी चाहिन्छ।
malāī pānī chāhincha.
I need water.
Sentence 6
यो किताब हो।
yo kitāb ho.
This is a book.
Sentence 7
यो मेरो घर हो।
yo mero ghar ho.
This is my house.
Sentence 8
म विद्यालय जान्छु।
ma vidyālay jānchu.
I go to school.

Level 3 — Everyday use

Read → understand → listen → echo → speak.

Sentence 9
उ मेरो साथी हो।
u mero sāthī ho.
He/She is my friend.
Sentence 10
म पानी पिउँछु।
ma pānī piũchu.
I drink water.
Sentence 11
यो रातो हो।
yo rāto ho.
This is red.
Sentence 12
धन्यवाद।
dhanyavād.
Thank you.

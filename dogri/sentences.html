Dogri — 12 Starter Sentences

There are now 12 lines, divided into three small levels so a beginner can build confidence gradually.

HomeAlphabetWords12 SentencesPractice7-Day Plan

How to practise each sentence

1. SeeLook at native script.
2. MeaningKnow what it says.
3. ListenPlay audio once.
4. EchoRepeat in chunks.
5. SpeakSay it without looking.

Level 1 — Greeting & identity

Read → understand → listen → echo → speak.

Sentence 1
नमस्ते।
namastē.
Hello.
Sentence 2
केह् हाल ऐ?
keh hāl ai?
How are you?
Sentence 3
अ'ऊं खरा आं।
āū̃ kharā ā̃.
I am fine.
Sentence 4
तुं'दा केह् नांऽ ऐ?
tundā keh nā̃ ai?
What is your name?

Level 2 — Needs & objects

Read → understand → listen → echo → speak.

Sentence 5
मेरा नांऽ ___ ऐ।
merā nā̃ ___ ai.
My name is ___.
Sentence 6
किरपा।
kirpā.
Please.
Sentence 7
शुक्रिया।
shukriyā.
Thank you.
Sentence 8
मिगी खिमा।
migī khimā.
Sorry.

Level 3 — Everyday use

Read → understand → listen → echo → speak.

Sentence 9
शौचाले कुत्थें ऐ?
shauchāle kutthē̃ ai?
Where is the toilet?
Sentence 10
रोको!
roko!
Stop!
Sentence 11
अग्ग!
agg!
Fire!
Sentence 12
मिगी इक्कला छोड़ी ओड़ो।
migi ikkalā chhoṛī oṛo.
Leave me alone.

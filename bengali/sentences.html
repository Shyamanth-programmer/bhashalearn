Bengali — 12 Starter Sentences

There are now 12 lines, divided into three small levels so a beginner can build confidence gradually.

HomeAlphabetWords12 SentencesPractice7-Day Plan

How to practise each sentence

1. SeeLook at native script.
2. MeaningKnow what it says.
3. ListenPlay audio once.
4. EchoRepeat in chunks.
5. SpeakSay it without looking.

Level 1 — Greeting & identity

Read → understand → listen → echo → speak.

Sentence 1
নমস্কার।
nomoshkar.
Hello.
Sentence 2
আমার নাম ___।
āmār nām ___.
My name is ___.
Sentence 3
তোমার নাম কী?
tomār nām kī?
What is your name?
Sentence 4
আমি ভালো আছি।
āmi bhālo āchi.
I am fine.

Level 2 — Needs & objects

Read → understand → listen → echo → speak.

Sentence 5
আমার জল চাই।
āmār jol chāi.
I want water.
Sentence 6
এটা বই।
eṭā boi.
This is a book.
Sentence 7
এটা আমার বাড়ি।
eṭā āmār bāṛi.
This is my home.
Sentence 8
আমি স্কুলে যাই।
āmi skule yāi.
I go to school.

Level 3 — Everyday use

Read → understand → listen → echo → speak.

Sentence 9
সে আমার বন্ধু।
se āmār bondhu.
He/She is my friend.
Sentence 10
আমি জল খাই।
āmi jol khāi.
I drink water.
Sentence 11
এটা লাল।
eṭā lāl.
This is red.
Sentence 12
ধন্যবাদ।
dhonnobād.
Thank you.
